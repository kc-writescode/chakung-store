# Chakung Store (`store.chakung.com`)

The organic village produce storefront for **Chakung**, Soreng district, West Sikkim (1,400m altitude).

## About Sikkim Organic Agriculture
In 2016, Sikkim became the **first 100% certified organic state in the world**, winning the UN FAO Future Policy Gold Award. All chemical fertilisers, synthetic pesticides, and GMO seeds are prohibited across the state.

## Products Showcased (13 Products)
1. **Dalle Khursani (Himalayan Cherry Chilli)** — GI Tagged, 100,000–350,000 SHU
2. **Turmeric (Besar)** — GI Tagged high-curcumin hill turmeric
3. **Gundruk (Fermented Greens)** — GI Tagged traditional probiotic condiment
4. **Buckwheat (Phapar)** — Gluten-free Tartary buckwheat from 1,400m+ ridges
5. **Soybean (Bhatmas)** — Non-GMO mountain soya, sun-dried on bamboo mats
6. **Rajma (Kidney Beans)** — Small highland speckled variety, creamy and slow-cooking
7. **Ginger (Adrak)** — GI Tagged pungent mountain ginger with citrus notes
8. **Mustard Seeds (Rayo ko Geda)** — Hillside black mustard seeds for tempering & raw oil
9. **Rice Bean (Masyam Dal)** — Purple-speckled native legume rich in protein & iron
10. **Maize Seed (Makai)** — High-altitude flint corn dried under village eaves
11. **Finger Millet (Kodo)** — Ancient calcium-dense grain for porridge, roti, and tongba
12. **Local Beans (Simi)** — Flat heirloom runner beans from bamboo garden trellises
13. **Pumpkin Seeds (Pharsi ko Geda)** — Sun-dried seeds from dense red mountain squash

## Tech Stack
- **Framework**: Next.js 15 (App Router)
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS with customized design tokens matching the Chakung brand identity
- **Typography**: Fraunces & Familjen Grotesk (Google Fonts)
- **Ordering**: Direct village WhatsApp ordering flow (`+91 62950 34036`)
